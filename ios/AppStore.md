## Description [4000]

¡Choca esos cinco! Choca Cinco is a Spanish flashcard app built around one idea: every word climbs five stages,
and each stage asks a little more of you than the last.

1. Aprender: meet the word, both sides showing.
2. Reconocer: see the Spanish, recall the English.
3. Recordar: see the English, say the Spanish out loud before you look.
4. Producir: type the Spanish. Accents count, but a near miss is marked, not failed.
5. Dominada: mastered. It comes back after three weeks, then less and less often.

Get it right and a card moves up and comes back later. Miss it and it drops one stage and returns a few cards
later in the same session.

THREE WAYS TO PRACTICE
• Vocabulario: words and their meanings.
• Conjugación: one card per verb per tense. Study the table, say it aloud, then type every form.
• Frases: whole expressions for real conversations, like ¿Dónde está el baño? and Me da igual.

YOUR WORDS, ADDED FAST
Start with 20 everyday words, 10 core verbs and 20 useful phrases, then build your own deck:
• Type a card by hand.
• Import a CSV file from the Files app.
• Use the built-in Shortcuts actions. The included guide sets up a shortcut that takes one word and fills in the
  meaning, an example, notes and, for verbs, every tense.
• Ask Siri: "Add a card to Choca Cinco".

MADE TO STICK
• Miss a card and write a quick memory trick. It shows with the answer next time.
• Hear every word, in a Latin American or Spain accent.
• The app itself is in Spanish, so you practice with every tap.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your cards and progress sync through your own iCloud, and everything works
offline.
